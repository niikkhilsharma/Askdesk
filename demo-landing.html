Questions visitors usually ask

Do you take new patients?

Yes. The first visit is a full checkup, and we ask you to arrive 15 minutes early.

Which insurance do you take?

Most major plans. Call the front desk if you want us to check yours before you come in.

Where do I park?

The lot behind the building is free for patients. Street parking is limited on weekdays.
